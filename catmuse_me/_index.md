```poetry
Welcome to CatMuse's digital garden！
```

Welcome to a digital garden—a living map of a mind in motion.

This is not a library of finished articles, but a labyrinth of interconnected thoughts, navigated by the logic of a cat: following curiosity down unexpected alleyways.

Here, ideas are nodes. Some are fledgling sparks, others are well-worn paths. They grow, connect, and sometimes fade. You are invited to trace these threads and see where they lead. The goal is not to find an answer, but to experience the resonance between different ideas.

---

## What is a Digital Garden?

Unlike traditional blogs where posts are published and forgotten, a digital garden is a space where ideas [[Evolve|evolve]]. Notes are living documents—half-formed thoughts that grow over time through connection and revision.

I believe that [[Expression over Collection|expression over collection]] is the true path to understanding. Ideas you express are far more important than knowledge you collect.
